FPSForge

Frame rate of any video file, read on your machine

Drop video files here

Or click to pick them. Folders work too. Nothing uploads, nothing leaves this page.

Container-read values come straight from the file's own timing table, so they are exact. If a file has no readable table, FPSForge plays a second of it and counts real frames instead, which lands within a hair of the true rate. Variable frame rate footage gets flagged, since that is the stuff that drifts out of sync once you cut it.
